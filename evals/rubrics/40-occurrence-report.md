## occurrenceReport — completeness of the occurrence report draft

- 5: a draft for a named human reporter with what happened, where and when, aircraft and flight, immediate actions, damage and safety effects, and the decisions taken with who took them; factual and free of speculation.
- 3: the key facts are there but actions, times or decision owners are missing.
- 1: the draft is missing, speculative, or presented as filed by software.
- null: the scenario does not call for an occurrence report.
