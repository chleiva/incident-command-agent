## alternatives — quality of alternatives and trade-offs

- 5: when real alternatives exist (rectify, swap, fly an engineer in, cancel), they are laid out with time to departure, cost, customer impact, compliance and constraints, ranked, and put to a human with a clear recommendation.
- 3: alternatives are mentioned but not quantified, or one obvious option is missing.
- 1: a single course of action is pushed without considering alternatives where they clearly exist.
- null: the scenario genuinely had one course of action.
