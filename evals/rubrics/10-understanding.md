## understanding — correct incident understanding

- 5: identifies the actual defect or event, its operational consequence (airworthiness, delay, downstream rotation, crew duty, passengers) and the constraints that matter, consistent with the reference summary.
- 3: the main problem is right but an important consequence or constraint is missed (for example the onward connections or the crew duty limit).
- 1: misreads the incident, or acts on the wrong aircraft, flight or station.
